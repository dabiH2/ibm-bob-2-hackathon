# CRA Compliance Mapping — `article14-gatekeeper`

> **Regulation:** (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act), OJ L, 20.11.2024  
> **Package:** `gatekeeper/` — Python release-gate that produces an evidence pack for every release candidate  
> **Mapping date:** based on codebase as read  
> **Note:** Art. 14 reporting obligations apply from **11 September 2026**; all other obligations from 11 December 2027.

---

## Summary of top 3 gaps

### Gap 1 — No public vulnerability disclosure artefact (Annex I Part II (4) + (8))
The law requires manufacturers to publicly disclose fixed-vulnerability information and to accompany security updates with advisory messages for users.  Gatekeeper produces internal VEX statements and draft Art. 14 reports, but nothing intended for public consumption.  **Next step:** auto-generate a CSAF 2.0 (or plain-Markdown) public advisory for every release that contains at least one `fixed` VEX statement, and add a gate check that it has been published.

### Gap 2 — No CVD policy validation (Annex I Part II (5) + Art. 13(8))
Manufacturers are required to have, document, and enforce a coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy, including a published contact address (Annex I Part II(6)) and a process for receiving external reports.  Gatekeeper never checks for the existence or contents of such a policy.  **Next step:** add a `security_policy` key to `gatekeeper.toml`; verify the referenced file exists, contains a contact address, and is committed to the repository; fail the gate if missing.

### Gap 3 — Upstream disclosure tracking absent (Art. 13(6))
When a vulnerable third-party component is identified, the CRA requires the manufacturer to notify the component maintainer and share the applied fix.  Gatekeeper identifies affected components and their fix versions in `gate.json` but never creates or tracks an upstream disclosure.  **Next step:** generate a per-finding `art13_6_upstream_disclosure.md` template (analogous to the Art. 14 drafts) for every `affected` or `fixed` finding, and add a `.submitted` flag in `.gatekeeper/upstream_disclosures.json` that the gate can check before declaring PASS.
